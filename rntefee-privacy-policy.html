資料儲存 Data Storage

您的記帳資料（物件、房間、房客備註、抄表度數、金額、電表照片）儲存在您的裝置上，並透過您自己的 iCloud（私人資料庫）在您的裝置之間同步。這些資料存放在您個人的 iCloud 帳戶中，開發者無法存取，也沒有任何自家伺服器收集或保存這些資料。

iCloud 共享：若您開啟「iCloud 共享」邀請他人一起管理，整份記帳資料（含電表照片）會存放在分享者的 iCloud 帳戶中，透過 Apple 的 iCloud 共享機制同步給受邀的成員；受邀成員可以檢視與修改這份資料。成員離開、被移除或分享者停止共享後，成員的裝置會回到成員自己的資料。開發者同樣無法存取共享的資料。每次切換資料前，App 會在您的裝置上留一份備份（設定 ▸ 本機備份），只存在您的裝置上。

Your bookkeeping data (properties, rooms, tenant notes, meter readings, amounts, and meter photos) is stored on your device and synced across your own devices through your personal iCloud (a private database). This data lives in your own iCloud account; the developer cannot access it, and runs no servers that collect or store it.

iCloud sharing: If you turn on iCloud sharing to manage the books together, the whole data set (including meter photos) is stored in the sharer's iCloud account and synced to the invited members through Apple's iCloud sharing; invited members can view and edit it. When a member leaves or is removed, or the sharer stops sharing, the member's devices return to the member's own data. The developer cannot access shared data either. Before switching data, the app keeps a backup on your device (Settings ▸ Local Backups) that stays only on your device.

我們收集或使用的資料 Data we collect / use

  • 廣告（Google Mobile Ads / AdMob）：為提供免費服務，App 會在部分畫面展示橫幅與插頁式（蓋板）廣告。廣告供應商可能蒐集裝置資訊（如廣告識別碼 IDFA、裝置型號、IP 位址）以投遞廣告、衡量成效及防止廣告詐欺。您可在 iOS【設定 ▸ 隱私權與安全性 ▸ 追蹤】管理追蹤許可，或重設廣告識別碼。解鎖 Pro 後即不再顯示廣告；Mac 版不顯示廣告。
  • Ads (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob): To keep the app free, it shows banner and interstitial ads on some screens. The ad provider may collect device info (advertising identifier IDFA, device model, IP address) for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. You can manage tracking in iOS Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking, or reset your advertising identifier. After unlocking Pro, no ads are shown; the Mac version shows no ads.
  • App 追蹤透明度（ATT）：App 首次啟動時會請求追蹤授權。若您拒絕，廣告商將自動改為非個人化廣告；App 功能不受影響。
  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On first launch, the app requests tracking authorization. If you decline, the ad provider automatically switches to non-personalized ads; app functionality is unaffected.
  • App 內購買（IAP）：透過 Apple App Store 解鎖「Pro」時，付款與交易記錄由 Apple 全權處理，開發者無法存取您的付款資訊。Mac 版的 30 天免費試用同樣以免費的 App 內購買項目記錄在您的 Apple 帳號。
  • In-App Purchases (IAP): When unlocking "Pro" through the Apple App Store, payment and transaction records are handled entirely by Apple. The developer cannot access your payment information. The Mac version's free 30-day trial is likewise recorded as a free in-app purchase on your Apple Account.
  • 相機與照片：當您為電表拍照或選取照片時，App 會請求相機／照片權限。電表照片僅儲存於您的裝置與您個人的 iCloud，不會上傳至開發者。
  • AI 辨識電表度數（Apple Intelligence）：自 1.1.1 版起，在 iOS 27 以上且支援 Apple Intelligence 的裝置上，「AI 辨識電表度數」會自動啟用（App 內沒有個別的關閉選項）：當您為電表拍照或選取照片時，App 會把縮小後的該張電表照片交給 Apple Intelligence 讀出度數。會優先使用 Apple 的 Private Cloud Compute（私密雲端運算）：照片由您的裝置直接傳送到 Apple 的伺服器處理，隨照片送出的只有一段固定的文字指示，不包含物件、房間、房客或金額等任何記帳資料。資料不經過開發者，開發者收不到、也無法存取這些照片或辨識結果。依 Apple 的說明，送往 Private Cloud Compute 的資料僅用於處理當次請求、不會被保留；其隱私與安全設計請參閱 Apple 的官方說明。當 Private Cloud Compute 無法使用時（例如沒有網路，或已達當日用量上限），只使用裝置端的文字辨識，照片不會離開您的裝置。
  • Camera & Photos: When you photograph a meter or pick a photo, the app requests camera/photo access. Meter photos are stored only on your device and in your own iCloud, and are never uploaded to the developer.
  • AI meter reading (Apple Intelligence): Starting with version 1.1.1, on devices running iOS 27 or later that support Apple Intelligence, "AI meter reading" is enabled automatically (there is no separate switch in the app): when you photograph a meter or pick a photo, the app hands a downscaled copy of that meter photo to Apple Intelligence to read the value. The app prefers Apple's Private Cloud Compute: the photo is sent from your device directly to Apple's servers for processing, together with only a fixed text instruction — no bookkeeping data such as properties, rooms, tenants or amounts. It never passes through the developer; the developer does not receive and cannot access these photos or the recognised values. According to Apple, data sent to Private Cloud Compute is used only to fulfil that request and is not retained; for its privacy and security design, see Apple's official description. When Private Cloud Compute is unavailable (for example, no network connection or the daily usage limit has been reached), only on-device text recognition is used and the photo does not leave your device.
  • 本機提醒通知：若您開啟「每月結算日提醒」，App 會在您的裝置上排定本機通知。這些提醒在裝置端排定，不會將任何資料傳送給開發者。
  • Local reminder notifications: If you enable the monthly settlement-day reminder, the app schedules local notifications on your device. These are scheduled on-device and send no data to the developer.
  • 捷徑與 Siri：App 提供捷徑動作（列出未收的電費、電費月總覽、標記已收取），在您的裝置上執行，不會傳送任何資料給開發者。
  • Shortcuts & Siri: The app offers Shortcuts actions (list unpaid bills, monthly summary, mark a bill collected). They run on your device and send no data to the developer.
  • Mac 版的 AI 助手連線（MCP）：Mac 版側邊欄的「AI 助手（MCP）」預設開啟（可隨時關閉）。開啟時，App 只在這台 Mac 的本機位址（127.0.0.1）提供連線，讓您在這台 Mac 上使用的 AI 工具讀取物件與帳單、記錄抄表度數或標記收款（不能修改物件、房間、房客與單價）；不對網路開放，資料也不經過開發者。AI 工具取得的資料，由您所使用的 AI 工具及其服務供應商依其隱私權政策處理；開發者收不到這些資料。您可隨時關閉此功能，關閉後既有連線也會立即中斷。
  • AI assistant connection on Mac (MCP): On by default in the Mac version under Settings ▸ AI Assistant Connection (you can turn it off at any time). When on, the app accepts connections only on this Mac's local address (127.0.0.1), so AI tools you use on this Mac can read properties and bills, record meter readings, or mark bills collected (they cannot change properties, rooms, tenants or rates). It is not reachable from the network and nothing passes through the developer. Data an AI tool obtains is handled by that tool and its provider under their own privacy policies; the developer does not receive it. You can turn this off at any time, which also closes existing connections immediately.

我們不會 We do not

  • 建立帳號或要求登入。
  • Create accounts or require sign-in.
  • 將您的記帳資料或電表照片上傳至開發者的伺服器（開發者沒有任何收集這些資料的伺服器）。這些資料只儲存在您的裝置與您自己的 iCloud（或您加入的共享之分享者的 iCloud）；唯一的例外是上述「AI 辨識電表度數」：在支援的裝置上，電表照片會交由 Apple 處理當次辨識。
  • Upload your bookkeeping data or meter photos to the developer (the developer runs no servers that collect them). This data is stored only on your device and in your own iCloud (or, for shared books, the sharer's iCloud); the one exception is "AI meter reading" above, which on supported devices hands a meter photo to Apple to process that single recognition.
  • 出售您的個人資料給第三方。
  • Sell your personal data to third parties.

資料保留與安全 Retention & Security

  • 您可隨時在 App 內「設定 ▸ 清除全部資料」移除裝置上的資料，或刪除 App。儲存在 iCloud 中的資料由您的 Apple ID 管理；共享的資料由分享者的 Apple ID 管理，成員可隨時在「設定 ▸ iCloud 共享」離開共享。
  • You can remove on-device data anytime via Settings ▸ Erase All Data, or delete the app. Data stored in iCloud is managed by your Apple ID; shared books are managed by the sharer's Apple ID, and members can leave at any time under Settings ▸ iCloud Sharing.
  • 廣告 SDK 的資料傳輸與安全由 Google 提供；iCloud 同步安全由 Apple 提供；IAP 交易安全由 Apple 提供。
  • Ad SDK data transmission and security are provided by Google; iCloud sync security by Apple; IAP transaction security by Apple.
