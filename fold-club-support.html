常見問題 FAQ FAQ

  • 一定要有 iPhone Duo 才能玩嗎？ 不用。一般 iPhone、iPad 都能玩大部分的遊戲；只有幾款用到摺疊角度的遊戲需要 iPhone Duo，在其他裝置上會另外放在「摺疊機限定」一區。
  • Do I need an iPhone Duo? No. Most games work on any iPhone or iPad. Only the few games that use the hinge angle need an iPhone Duo; on other devices they are listed in a separate “Foldable only” section.
  • Pro 包含什麼？ 一次買斷（不是訂閱）：解鎖標示 Pro 的遊戲，並拿掉大廳裡的廣告。支援家人共享，Apple Watch 上同一個 Apple 帳號也會一起解鎖。
  • What does Pro include? A one-time purchase (not a subscription) that unlocks the games marked Pro and removes the ads in the lobby. It supports Family Sharing, and also unlocks on an Apple Watch using the same Apple Account.
  • 買過了但還是鎖著？ 到購買畫面點「回復購買」。請確認裝置登入的是購買時的 Apple 帳號，並且連得上網路。
  • I bought Pro but games are still locked. Open the purchase screen and tap “Restore purchase”. Make sure the device is signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase and is online.
  • 怎麼跟朋友連線對戰？ 兩台以上的裝置都登入 Game Center，在大廳點連線對戰、找對手或邀請朋友。2 到 4 人一桌，由主揪選遊戲；只有每個人都玩得了的遊戲會出現在清單裡。
  • How do I play online with friends? Sign in to Game Center on each device, open Play Online from the lobby, then find opponents or invite friends. A table seats 2 to 4 players and the host picks the game. Only games that everyone at the table can play are listed.
  • 連線顯示登入失敗？ 請到 iOS【設定 ▸ Game Center】確認已登入，再回到 App 重試。
  • Online says sign-in failed. Check that you are signed in under iOS Settings ▸ Game Center, then return to the App and try again.
  • 會顯示廣告嗎？ 免費版只在大廳的遊戲清單裡顯示廣告，遊戲進行中沒有；購買 Pro 後不再顯示。
  • Does the app show ads? The free version shows ads only in the game list in the lobby, never during a game. Ads are removed after you buy Pro.
  • 彈珠台一直偏一邊？ 彈珠台會跟著手機傾斜。想固定的話，在遊戲畫面把「會傾斜」切成「不傾斜」。
  • The pinball keeps drifting to one side. The table follows the tilt of your phone. To keep it level, switch “Tilt on” to “Tilt off” on the game screen.
