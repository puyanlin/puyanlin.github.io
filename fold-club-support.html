玩什麼What's in the arcade

立體的機台Chunky 3D machines

  • 夾娃娃機：五枚硬幣，看準影子再下爪。
  • 夜市彈珠台：把手機歪一邊，彈珠真的會跟著偏。
  • 吧台滑杯：一推，停得離杯墊越近越高分。
  • 疊啤酒箱、趕打卡、老闆來了……每一台都是看得出厚度的 3D 小模型。
  • Claw Machine: five coins. Line up the shadow, then drop.
  • Night Pinball: tilt your phone and the marble really drifts.
  • Bar Slide: push the mug and stop it closest to the coaster.
  • Crate Stacker, Clock-In Dash, Boss Alert… every cabinet is a little 3D model.

考眼力、考手速Sharp eyes, fast hands

  • 找碴：兩個櫃子，哪裡不一樣？
  • 點鈔機：一疊鈔票混著假貨，十五秒數對幾張。
  • 下班打磚塊、打小人：把待辦和討厭鬼一口氣清掉。
  • 藍白拖打蟑螂：地上的拖鞋拍下去，飛起來的趕快擊落。
  • Spot the Swap: two cabinets. What changed?
  • Cash Counter: fakes in the stack. How many can you count in fifteen seconds?
  • To-Do Smasher and Whack a Jerk: clear out today's to-dos, and today's jerks.
  • Slipper vs. Roaches: a slipper for the ones on the floor, a swat for the ones that fly.

摺疊機更好玩Better on a foldable

  • 角度大師：說 87 度就 87 度，憑手感摺到指定角度。
  • 拍蚊子：蚊子停下來的那一瞬間，把手機闔上，啪。
  • 貪食蛇：內外螢幕各是一面，闔上手機，蛇就鑽到另一面。
  • 半開立在桌上時，畫面會避開摺線。一般手機也能玩其餘所有遊戲。
  • Angle Master: asked for 87 degrees? Fold to exactly that, by feel.
  • Mosquito Clap: the moment it lands, snap your phone shut.
  • Snake: one side on each screen. Fold shut and the snake burrows through.
  • Half open on a table, the screen splits around the hinge. Every other game plays on any regular phone.

揪人尬一場Play online

開一桌拿四位數房號，朋友輸入房號進來，2 到 4 個人湊一桌。主揪點遊戲，大家玩一模一樣的關卡，分數最高的拿一勝。不管朋友用哪一種手機，都能進同一桌。每款遊戲都有排行榜。

Open a table, share the four-digit room code, and 2 to 4 players join. The host picks a game, everyone plays the exact same level, and the top score takes the round. Whatever phone your friends have, they can join the same table. Every game has its own leaderboard.

雙面貪食蛇Two-sided Snake

在摺疊機上，闔上手機，蛇就從內螢幕鑽到外螢幕；打開，又鑽回草地。沒有摺疊機也能玩：一般手機鑽進洞，木盤自己翻面。

On a foldable, fold shut and the snake burrows from the inner screen to the outer one; open up and it pops back onto the grass. No foldable? On a regular phone it dives into a hole and the board flips over by itself.
