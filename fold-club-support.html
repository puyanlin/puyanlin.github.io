玩什麼What's in the arcade

立體的機台Chunky 3D machines

  • 夾娃娃機：五枚硬幣，看準影子再下爪。
  • 夜市彈珠台：把手機歪一邊，彈珠真的會跟著偏。
  • 吧台滑杯：一推，停得離杯墊越近越高分。
  • 疊啤酒箱、趕打卡、老闆來了……每一台都是看得出厚度的 3D 小模型。
  • Claw Machine: five coins. Line up the shadow, then drop.
  • Night Pinball: tilt your phone and the marble really drifts.
  • Bar Slide: push the mug and stop it closest to the coaster.
  • Crate Stacker, Clock-In Dash, Boss Alert… every cabinet is a little 3D model.

考眼力、考手速Sharp eyes, fast hands

  • 找碴：兩個櫃子，哪裡不一樣？
  • 點鈔機：一疊鈔票混著假貨，十五秒數對幾張。
  • 下班打磚塊、打小人：把待辦和討厭鬼一口氣清掉。
  • 藍白拖打蟑螂：地上的拖鞋拍下去，飛起來的趕快擊落。
  • Spot the Swap: two cabinets. What changed?
  • Cash Counter: fakes in the stack. How many can you count in fifteen seconds?
  • To-Do Smasher and Whack a Jerk: clear out today's to-dos, and today's jerks.
  • Slipper vs. Roaches: a slipper for the ones on the floor, a swat for the ones that fly.

摺疊機更好玩Better on a foldable

  • 角度大師：說 87 度就 87 度，憑手感摺到指定角度。
  • 拍蚊子：蚊子停下來的那一瞬間，把手機闔上，啪。
  • 貪食蛇：內外螢幕各是一面，闔上手機，蛇就鑽到另一面。
  • 半開立在桌上時，畫面會避開摺線。一般手機也能玩其餘所有遊戲。
  • Angle Master: asked for 87 degrees? Fold to exactly that, by feel.
  • Mosquito Clap: the moment it lands, snap your phone shut.
  • Snake: one side on each screen. Fold shut and the snake burrows through.
  • Half open on a table, the screen splits around the hinge. Every other game plays on any regular phone.

揪人尬一場Play online

開一桌拿四位數房號，朋友輸入房號進來，2 到 4 個人湊一桌。主揪點遊戲，大家玩一模一樣的關卡，分數最高的拿一勝。不管朋友用哪一種手機，都能進同一桌。每款遊戲都有排行榜。

Open a table, share the four-digit room code, and 2 to 4 players join. The host picks a game, everyone plays the exact same level, and the top score takes the round. Whatever phone your friends have, they can join the same table. Every game has its own leaderboard.

雙面貪食蛇Two-sided Snake

在摺疊機上，闔上手機，蛇就從內螢幕鑽到外螢幕；打開，又鑽回草地。沒有摺疊機也能玩：一般手機鑽進洞，木盤自己翻面。

On a foldable, fold shut and the snake burrows from the inner screen to the outer one; open up and it pops back onto the grass. No foldable? On a regular phone it dives into a hole and the board flips over by itself.

常見問題FAQ

一定要有摺疊機才能玩嗎？Do I need a foldable phone?

不用。一般手機和 iPad 都能玩大部分的遊戲；只有幾款用到摺疊角度的遊戲要在摺得起來的手機上玩，在其他裝置上會放在大廳最下面。

No. Most games play on any phone or iPad. Only the few games that use the hinge angle need a foldable; on other devices they're listed at the bottom of the lobby.

Pro 包含什麼？What does Pro include?

一次買斷（不是訂閱）：解鎖 Pro 限定的遊戲和連線對戰，並拿掉大廳裡的廣告。在 App Store 購買支援家人共享，同一個 Apple 帳號的 Apple Watch 也會一起解鎖。

A one-time purchase (not a subscription) that unlocks the Pro-only games and online play, and removes the ads in the lobby. Purchases on the App Store support Family Sharing and also unlock on an Apple Watch using the same Apple Account.

買過了但還是鎖著？I bought Pro but games are still locked.

到購買畫面點「回復購買」。請確認裝置登入的是購買時的帳號（iPhone、iPad 是 Apple 帳號，Android 是 Google 帳號），並且連得上網路。

Open the purchase screen and tap “Restore purchase”. Make sure the device is signed in to the account you bought it with (your Apple Account on iPhone and iPad, your Google account on Android) and is online.

怎麼跟朋友連線對戰？How do I play online with friends?

在大廳右上角點雙人圖示，開一桌拿到四位數房號，朋友在同一頁輸入房號就能加入。2 到 4 人一桌，主揪點遊戲；只有每個人都玩得了的遊戲會出現在清單裡。連線對戰是 Pro 的功能，要加入的每個人都需要 Pro。

Tap the two-people icon at the top right of the lobby and open a table to get a four-digit room code. Friends enter the code on the same screen to join. A table seats 2 to 4 players and the host picks the game; only games everyone at the table can play are listed. Online play is part of Pro, so everyone who joins needs Pro.

排行榜在哪裡看？Where are the leaderboards?

每款遊戲都有排行榜：iPhone、iPad 用 Game Center，Android 用 Google Play 遊戲。

Every game has a leaderboard: Game Center on iPhone and iPad, Google Play Games on Android.

會顯示廣告嗎？Does the app show ads?

免費版只在大廳的遊戲清單裡顯示廣告，遊戲進行中沒有；購買 Pro 後不再顯示。

The free version shows ads only in the game list in the lobby, never during a game. Ads are removed after you buy Pro.

彈珠台一直偏一邊？The pinball keeps drifting to one side.

彈珠台會跟著手機傾斜。想固定的話，在遊戲畫面把「會傾斜」切成「不傾斜」。

The table follows the tilt of your phone. To keep it level, switch “Tilt on” to “Tilt off” on the game screen.
