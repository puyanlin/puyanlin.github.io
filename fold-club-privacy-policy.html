不摺手段 Fold Club – 隱私權政策 Privacy Policy

最後更新 Last Updated: 2026-Nov-13

中文版

「不摺手段」（Fold Club）是一款小遊戲合輯，重視您的隱私。本政策說明 App 會用到哪些資料、交給誰處理。

我們收集或使用的資料

  • 廣告（Google Mobile Ads / AdMob）：免費版在遊戲大廳的清單裡會顯示廣告，遊戲進行中沒有廣告。廣告供應商 Google 可能蒐集裝置識別碼（您允許追蹤時才包含 IDFA）、IP 位址（用來推估大略地區）、裝置型號與系統版本、廣告的顯示與點擊紀錄，以及診斷資料，用於投遞與衡量廣告、防止不正常流量。第一次載入廣告前，App 會依 iOS 的規定詢問是否允許追蹤；不允許也能照常遊玩，只是廣告不會依您的興趣投放。您可以隨時在 iOS【設定 ▸ 隱私權與安全性 ▸ 追蹤】變更。購買 Pro 之後不再顯示廣告，也不會再詢問追蹤。詳見 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站與 App 的資料。
  • Game Center / GameKit：用於排行榜。您的 Game Center 暱稱與成績由 Apple 處理並顯示在排行榜上；開發者拿不到您的 Apple 帳號資料。不登入 Game Center 仍可遊玩。
  • 連線對戰（Google Firebase）：Pro 的連線對戰使用 Google 的 Firebase（匿名登入與即時資料庫，資料存放在新加坡），跟 Android 版是同一個服務。開桌或進房間時，App 會匿名登入並取得一組隨機的使用者代碼，不需要帳號、電子郵件或電話；連線時也會附上 Apple 的 App Attest 證明，讓服務確認是正版的 App（不含個人資料）。暱稱預設是您的 Game Center 名字（沒登入時是「玩家」加三位數字），可以自己改。您的暱稱、所在的房間與每回合的成績會暫時存在資料庫裡，讓同一桌的玩家看得到；您離開房間或斷線時，您的資料會從房間移除，所有人都離開後整個房間會被清掉。不玩連線對戰就不會用到這項服務。
  • Apple Intelligence：在支援的裝置上，結算畫面的一句講評與「打小人」的小人稱號由 Apple 的語言模型產生（在裝置端，或經由 Apple 的 Private Cloud Compute）。送給模型的只有遊戲名稱、成績數字與是否破紀錄，不含任何個人資料；產生的文字不會儲存或回傳給開發者。裝置不支援或暫時無法使用時，App 會改用內建的句子。
  • App 內購買：「Pro」由 Apple 的 App Store 處理付款。App 只會得知「是否已購買」，拿不到您的付款資料。若您有配對的 Apple Watch，這個購買狀態會在您自己的 iPhone 與手錶之間同步。
  • 動作感測與摺疊角度：部分遊戲會讀取裝置的傾斜方向，或 iPhone Duo 的開合角度來操作遊戲。這些數值只在裝置上即時使用，不會儲存，也不會傳出裝置。
  • 遊戲紀錄：最佳成績與設定（例如靜音）儲存在您的裝置上；登入 Game Center 時，最佳成績會送到 Apple 的排行榜。

Android 版

Android 版沒有 Game Center、Apple Intelligence 與手錶，以下幾項跟上面不同：

  • 廣告（Google Mobile Ads / AdMob）：同樣只在大廳的清單裡顯示。Google 可能蒐集廣告 ID、IP 位址（用來推估大略地區）、裝置型號與系統版本、廣告的顯示與點擊紀錄，以及診斷資料。在法規要求的地區（例如歐洲經濟區與英國），第一次載入廣告前會出現 Google 的同意表單，之後可以從大廳最下面的「廣告隱私設定」更改；您也可以在 Android【設定 ▸ Google ▸ 廣告】重設或刪除廣告 ID。購買 Pro 之後不再顯示廣告。
  • 連線對戰（Google Firebase）：Pro 的連線對戰使用 Google 的 Firebase（匿名登入與即時資料庫，資料存放在新加坡）。開桌或進房間時，App 會匿名登入並取得一組隨機的使用者代碼，不需要帳號、電子郵件或電話。您自己取的暱稱、所在的房間與每回合的成績會暫時存在資料庫裡，讓同一桌的玩家看得到；您離開房間或斷線時，您的資料會從房間移除，所有人都離開後整個房間會被清掉。不玩連線對戰就不會用到這項服務。
  • 排行榜（Google Play Games）：如果您的裝置登入了 Play Games 個人資料，每局結束時 App 會把那一款的分數送到 Google Play Games 的排行榜。Google 會得知您的 Play Games 玩家代號、玩家名稱與頭像，以及分數；分數要不要讓其他人看到，由您在 Play Games 個人資料的隱私設定決定。沒有登入 Play Games 就不會送出任何分數。您可以在 Play Games 個人資料刪除這款遊戲的資料。
  • App 內購買：「Pro」由 Google Play 處理付款。App 只會得知「是否已購買」，拿不到您的付款資料。
  • 動作感測與摺疊角度：部分遊戲會讀取裝置的傾斜方向或摺疊機的開合角度，只在裝置上即時使用，不會儲存，也不會傳出裝置。
  • 刪除資料：刪除 App 即可移除裝置上的遊戲紀錄、暱稱與匿名代碼；連線房間裡的資料在您離開時就會移除。需要協助確認或刪除時，請來信。

我們不會

  • 建立帳號或要求您提供姓名、電子郵件、電話等個人資料。
  • 把您的資料上傳到開發者自有的伺服器（只有上面說的連線對戰會用到 Firebase）。
  • 使用相機、麥克風、照片、通訊錄或精確位置。
  • 發送推播通知。

資料保留與刪除

  • 刪除 App 即可移除儲存在裝置上的遊戲紀錄、設定與暱稱；連線房間裡的資料在您離開時就會移除。
  • 排行榜上的成績由 Apple 的 Game Center 保存，可在 iOS【設定 ▸ Game Center】管理。
  • 廣告相關資料由 Google 依其隱私權政策保存與處理。

English

Fold Club (“the App”) is a collection of minigames. This policy explains what data the App uses and who handles it.

Data we collect or use

  • Ads (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob): The free version shows ads in the game list in the lobby. There are no ads during gameplay. The ad provider, Google, may collect device identifiers (the IDFA only if you allow tracking), your IP address (used to estimate a general region), device model and OS version, ad impressions and taps, and diagnostic data, in order to deliver and measure ads and to prevent invalid traffic. Before the first ad loads, the App asks for tracking permission as required by iOS. You can decline and still play; ads will simply not be personalised. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking. After you buy Pro, ads are no longer shown and the tracking prompt no longer appears. See how Google uses data from sites and apps that use its services.
  • Game Center / GameKit: Used for leaderboards. Your Game Center nickname and scores are handled by Apple and shown on leaderboards. The developer cannot access your Apple account information. You can play without signing in to Game Center.
  • Online play (Google Firebase): Online play, a Pro feature, uses Google Firebase (Anonymous Authentication and Realtime Database, hosted in Singapore), the same service the Android version uses. When you open or join a table, the App signs in anonymously and receives a random user ID. No account, email or phone number is needed. The App also attaches an Apple App Attest attestation so the service can confirm it is the genuine App; it contains no personal data. Your nickname starts as your Game Center name (or “Player” plus three digits if you are not signed in), and you can change it. Your nickname, the room you are in and each round’s score are stored temporarily so that players at the same table can see them. Your entry is removed when you leave the room or disconnect, and the whole room is cleared once everyone has left. If you never play online, this service is not used.
  • Apple Intelligence: On supported devices, the one-line remark on the result screen and the nicknames in “Whack a Jerk” are generated by Apple’s language model (on device, or through Apple’s Private Cloud Compute). Only the game name, the score and whether it is a new record are sent to the model. No personal data is included, and the generated text is not stored or sent to the developer. When the feature is unavailable, the App uses built-in lines instead.
  • In-app purchase: “Pro” is paid for through Apple’s App Store. The App only learns whether the purchase exists and never receives your payment details. If you have a paired Apple Watch, that purchase status is synced between your own iPhone and watch.
  • Motion and fold angle: Some games read the device’s tilt, or the hinge angle of iPhone Duo, as game input. These values are used on the device in real time. They are not stored and never leave the device.
  • Game records: Best scores and settings (such as mute) are stored on your device. When you are signed in to Game Center, best scores are submitted to Apple’s leaderboards.

Android version

The Android version has no Game Center, Apple Intelligence or watch app. These items differ from the above:

  • Ads (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob): Ads also appear only in the game list in the lobby. Google may collect the advertising ID, your IP address (used to estimate a general region), device model and OS version, ad impressions and taps, and diagnostic data. Where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area and the UK), Google’s consent form appears before the first ad loads, and you can change your choice later from “Ad privacy settings” at the bottom of the lobby. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in Android Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads. After you buy Pro, ads are no longer shown.
  • Online play (Google Firebase): Online play, a Pro feature, uses Google Firebase (Anonymous Authentication and Realtime Database, hosted in Singapore). When you open or join a table, the App signs in anonymously and receives a random user ID. No account, email or phone number is needed. The nickname you choose, the room you are in and each round’s score are stored temporarily so that players at the same table can see them. Your entry is removed when you leave the room or disconnect, and the whole room is cleared once everyone has left. If you never play online, this service is not used.
  • Leaderboards (Google Play Games): If your device is signed in to a Play Games profile, the App submits your score for that game to its Google Play Games leaderboard when a round ends. Google receives your Play Games player ID, player name and avatar, and the score. Whether other people can see your scores is controlled by the privacy settings of your Play Games profile. Nothing is submitted if you are not signed in to Play Games. You can delete this game’s data from your Play Games profile.
  • In-app purchase: “Pro” is paid for through Google Play. The App only learns whether the purchase exists and never receives your payment details.
  • Motion and fold angle: Some games read the device’s tilt or a foldable’s hinge angle. These values are used on the device in real time and are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Deleting data: Deleting the App removes the scores, nickname and anonymous ID stored on the device. Data in an online room is removed when you leave. If you need help confirming or deleting anything, email us.

We do not

  • Create accounts or ask for personal details such as your name, email address or phone number.
  • Upload your data to developer-run servers (only the online play described above uses Firebase).
  • Use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • Send push notifications.

Retention and deletion

  • Deleting the App removes the game records, settings and nickname stored on your device. Data in an online room is removed when you leave.
  • Leaderboard scores are kept by Apple’s Game Center and can be managed in iOS Settings ▸ Game Center.
  • Advertising data is retained and processed by Google under its privacy policy.
